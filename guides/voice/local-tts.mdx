Local TTS (text-to-speech) lets Tavo speak character responses without calling any external API. The synthesis runs entirely on your Android device using a TTS engine you install locally. This means voice works even without an internet connection and there are no per-character API costs.

Tavo supports two local TTS engines:

- **Google TTS** (`com.google.android.tts`) — Google's on-device synthesis engine, available through the Google Play Store. Supports many languages and downloadable offline voice packs.
- **iFlytek TTS** (`com.iflytek.speechcloud` or `com.iflytek.speechsuite`) — A high-quality Chinese TTS engine that runs fully offline. Supports Android 11 and above.

<Note>
Local TTS is only available on Android. iOS devices use the system speech engine differently and are not covered by this guide.
</Note>

## Limitations compared to cloud TTS

| | Local TTS | Cloud TTS (ElevenLabs, Gemini, etc.) |
|---|---|---|
| Internet required | No | Yes |
| Voice expressiveness | Moderate | High |
| Language support | Depends on installed voice pack | Broad, provider-dependent |
| Per-use cost | Free | Depends on provider plan |
| Voice customization per character | Limited to installed voices | Full voice library from provider |

## Setting up Google TTS

Google TTS requires the full Google Services suite to be installed on your device. If your device already has Google Play Store and the Google TTS engine (Speech Recognition & Synthesis by Google LLC), you can skip Part 1 and go straight to Part 2.

### Part 1: Install Google Services (if not already present)

Some Android devices — particularly those sold in regions without Google services, or running AOSP-based ROMs — do not include Google Play out of the box. Choose the method that fits your situation:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Manual APK (recommended for beginners)">
    This method works for most Android devices and does not require root access.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Install Google Services Framework">
        Download the appropriate version for your device from [APKMirror — Google Services Framework](https://www.apkmirror.com/apk/google-inc/google-services-framework/). Choose a stable release that matches your Android version. Install the APK. No icon will appear on your home screen — that is expected.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Install Google Play Services">
        Download from [APKMirror — Google Play Services](https://www.apkmirror.com/apk/google-inc/google-play-services/) and install. Again, no home screen icon will appear.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Install Google Play Store">
        Download from [APKMirror — Google Play Store](https://www.apkmirror.com/apk/google-inc/google-play-store/) and install. If the app shows a black screen or crashes, try an older compatible version.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Warning>
      Only download APKs from APKMirror or other trusted sources. Verify the developer is listed as **Google LLC** before installing.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Open GApps flash (advanced)">
    This method flashes Google services at the system level and requires an unlocked bootloader and a custom recovery environment (such as TWRP).

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Download an Open GApps package">
        Visit [https://opengapps.org](https://opengapps.org) and download the package that matches your device architecture (for example, ARM64) and Android version.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Copy the ZIP to your device">
        Place the downloaded ZIP file in your device's root storage. Do not extract it.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Boot into recovery">
        Power off your device, then hold the correct key combination to enter your custom recovery.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Flash the package">
        In the recovery interface, select **Install → Choose zip from /sdcard**, locate the GApps ZIP, and confirm the flash.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Reboot">
        After flashing completes, reboot your device. Your existing data is preserved.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Warning>
      Select the Open GApps package that exactly matches your device architecture and Android version. An incompatible package can prevent the device from booting.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Sandbox app (Huawei / HarmonyOS)">
    Huawei and HarmonyOS devices cannot run Google services directly. Use a sandbox application to run them in an isolated virtual environment.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Download a sandbox app">
        Search your device's app store for a virtual space or sandbox application (such as "出境易" or a similar tool).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Create a virtual space">
        Open the sandbox app and create a new isolated environment.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Install Google services inside the sandbox">
        Inside the virtual space, follow the Manual APK steps above to install Google Services Framework, Google Play Services, and Google Play Store.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Samsung (Chinese domestic models)">
    Some Samsung Galaxy devices sold in China include hidden Google service support that can be activated without any additional APKs.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Enable Google Services">
        Open **Settings → Accounts and backup → Manage accounts** and turn on **Google Services**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Update Google Play Store">
        Use a third-party app store (such as APKPure) to download and install the latest version of Google Play Store.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Verify">
        After completing these steps, Google Play Store should appear on your home screen and you can sign in normally.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Part 2: Install the Google TTS engine

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Google Play Store">
    Confirm you are signed in with your Google account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for the engine">
    In the search bar, type **Speech Recognition & Synthesis**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the official app">
    Find the app published by **Google LLC** and tap **Install**. No home screen icon will appear after installation — this is expected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it in Tavo">
    Open Tavo and go to **More → Voice → Voice API → New**. Set the provider to **System TTS**. In the model list, look for `com.google.android.tts`. In **My Voices**, tap the voice you want to preview.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Adjusting speech rate for System TTS

If voices sound too fast or too slow, you can adjust the speech rate at the system level on Android — Tavo uses whatever rate your system TTS is configured to use.

Open your device **Settings**, search for **Text-to-speech output** or **Text-to-speech**, and adjust the **Speech rate** and **Pitch** sliders.

## Setting up iFlytek TTS

iFlytek TTS is a professional Chinese-language synthesis engine that works fully offline. It requires **Android 11 or later**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the iFlytek TTS engine">
    Download the ZIP archive from the following URL:

    ```
    https://gitcode.com/open-source-toolkit/de7bc/blob/main/讯飞语音引擎.zip
    ```

    The archive contains two APKs:

    - **32-bit** — For older or 32-bit devices
    - **64-bit** — For modern Android devices (recommended)

    If you are unsure which to use, try the 64-bit version first. Most devices released after 2018 support 64-bit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow installation from unknown sources">
    Go to **Settings → Security → Install unknown apps** and grant permission to your file manager or browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the APK">
    Extract the ZIP and tap the APK file for your architecture. Follow the on-screen prompts to complete installation. No home screen icon will appear — that is normal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it in Tavo">
    Open Tavo and go to **More → Voice → Voice API → New**. Set the provider to **System TTS**. In the model list, look for `com.iflytek.speechcloud` or `com.iflytek.speechsuite`. Tap a voice in **My Voices** to preview it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
iFlytek TTS requires Android 11 or above. Installation will fail silently on older versions. Confirm your Android version in **Settings → About phone** before proceeding.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The TTS engine does not appear in Tavo's model list">
    - Confirm the APK installed successfully (check Settings → Apps to verify it appears there).
    - Restart Tavo after installation; the model list refreshes on launch.
    - For iFlytek, confirm your device runs Android 11 or later.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Voice plays but the speed is wrong">
    Adjust the speech rate in your Android system TTS settings. Search for "Text-to-speech" in your device Settings and change the speed slider.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Google Play Store crashes after installation">
    The installed version may not be compatible with your device. Uninstall it and try an older stable version from APKMirror.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Google services fail to connect">
    - Verify your device's clock is set to the correct date and time (sync errors can block Google authentication).
    - Make sure all three Google components (Services Framework, Play Services, Play Store) are installed and up to date.
    - Restart the device after installation to let the services initialize fully.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
