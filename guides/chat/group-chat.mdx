Group chat lets you add multiple AI characters to a single conversation. You can mix and match characters, adjust who speaks and when, and use `@` mentions to direct questions at specific characters.

## Start a group chat

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu bar">
    Tap the menu icon on the left side of the screen to open the side panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the + button and select Start Group Chat">
    Tap the **+** icon at the top of the panel. In the menu that appears, select **Start Group Chat**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose characters">
    Browse your character list and select every character you want to include. Confirm your selection to open the new group chat session.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Manage group members

You can add or remove characters from a group chat at any time without losing the conversation history.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the right sidebar">
    Inside the group chat, tap the sidebar icon in the top-right corner of the screen to open the settings panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Group Members section">
    Scroll to the **Group Members** area at the top of the panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add or remove members">
    - To **add** a character, tap **Add** and select from your character list.
    - To **remove** a character, tap the **×** button on the right side of that member's entry.
    - To **mute** a character so they stop responding, tap the message icon on the right side of their entry. Tap it again to unmute.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Muting a character keeps them in the group but prevents them from sending any messages. This is useful if you want to temporarily exclude a character without removing them entirely.
</Note>

## Set the reply mode

Reply modes control how characters respond after you send a message. You can change the mode at any time from the sidebar panel.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the right sidebar">
    Tap the sidebar icon in the top-right corner to open the panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll to Reply Mode">
    Scroll down in the panel to find the **Reply Mode** setting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a mode">
    Select the mode that fits how you want the conversation to flow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Natural chat">
    **Natural chat** is the default mode. If your message mentions a character by name using `@`, that character responds first. If no character is mentioned, a random character in the group replies.

    This mode produces the most organic, conversation-like feel.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="All reply">
    **All reply** mode causes every character in the group to respond to each message you send. Use this when you want to compare perspectives or hear from every character at once.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Directed reply">
    **Directed reply** mode means no character responds automatically. You must use `@CharacterName` in your message to explicitly choose who speaks next. This gives you precise control over the conversation flow.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

---

## Address a specific character with @

In any reply mode, you can direct a message to a specific character by mentioning them with `@`.

Type `@` followed by the character's name in your message. In **Natural chat** mode, the mentioned character responds first. In **Directed reply** mode, only the mentioned character responds.

---

## Quick-select who speaks next

You can instantly assign the next speaking turn to any character by tapping their avatar in the character row at the top of the chat screen. The selected character will be prompted to respond to your next message.
