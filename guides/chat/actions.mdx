Tavo gives you full control over your chat sessions. From the menu bar and the in-chat sidebar, you can organize conversations, inspect usage statistics, and manipulate individual messages to shape the story the way you want.

## Filter chats by character

When you have many conversations, the character filter helps you quickly find chats with a specific character.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu bar">
    Tap the menu icon on the left to open the **All Chats** list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a character name">
    Tap the character whose conversations you want to view. The list updates to show only chats involving that character; all others are temporarily hidden.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Rename a chat

Give any conversation a meaningful name so it is easy to find later.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the chat">
    Navigate to the conversation you want to rename.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the conversation name">
    At the top of the chat screen, tap the current conversation name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a new name">
    Type the new name and confirm to save it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Pin a chat to the top

Pinning keeps an important conversation at the top of the list regardless of message activity.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu bar">
    Tap the menu icon to open the chat list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the chat options">
    Find the conversation you want to pin and tap the **⋮** (more options) button on the right side of that entry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Pin">
    Tap **Pin** in the action menu. The conversation moves to the top of the list for that character and stays there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Clone a chat

Cloning creates an independent copy of a conversation — complete history included — so you can explore a different direction without affecting the original.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu bar">
    Tap the menu icon to open the chat list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the chat options">
    Find the conversation you want to clone and tap the **⋮** button on its right side.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Clone chat">
    Tap **Clone Chat**. A new conversation containing the full history appears in your chat list immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Cloning is useful when you want to try a different response or story branch. Make your changes in the clone and keep the original intact.
</Tip>

---

## Delete a chat

<Warning>
  Deleting a conversation permanently removes it and all its messages. This action cannot be undone.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu bar">
    Tap the menu icon to open the chat list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the chat options">
    Find the conversation you want to delete and tap the **⋮** button on its right side.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm deletion">
    Tap **Delete**. A confirmation dialog appears — tap to confirm and the conversation is permanently removed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Restart the current chat

Restarting clears the entire message history and opens a new blank session with the same character and settings.

<Warning>
  The current chat history is permanently deleted when you restart. Character settings, model parameters, and system prompts remain unchanged.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the right sidebar">
    Inside the chat screen, tap the sidebar icon in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll to the bottom of the panel">
    Swipe down to the bottom of the sidebar menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Restart Chat">
    Tap **Restart Chat**. A new empty session opens immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## View conversation statistics

The statistics panel shows a summary of your activity in the current conversation.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the right sidebar">
    Inside the chat screen, tap the sidebar icon in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll to the bottom and tap Statistics">
    Swipe down to the bottom of the sidebar panel and tap **Statistics**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The statistics page displays:

| Metric | Description |
|---|---|
| **Days together** | Total number of days since the conversation was created. |
| **Message count** | Number of messages sent by you and by the character. |
| **Total characters** | Cumulative character count across the entire conversation. |

## View all chats with a character

If you want to browse every conversation you have ever had with a specific character, you can access that list from the character's profile.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Characters page">
    Open the menu bar, scroll to the bottom, and tap **Characters**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the character's options">
    Find the character you are interested in and tap the **⋮** button on its entry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Chat history">
    Tap **Chat History**. The page shows every past conversation with that character. Tap any entry to open the full chat.
  </Step>
</Steps>
