As you use Tavo, it accumulates cached audio files, application logs, and character images that can add up over time. The Storage Space screen gives you a clear breakdown of what is using space and lets you clean up safely without touching your conversations or settings.

## Opening the storage settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sidebar">
    Swipe right or tap the menu icon to open the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap More, then Settings">
    Tap **More** at the bottom of the sidebar, then select **Settings** from the expanded menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Storage Space">
    Inside Settings, tap the **Storage Space** tab to view the storage overview.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understanding storage categories

The Storage Space screen shows the total space Tavo is currently using, followed by a list of data categories you can selectively clear.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Cache">
    Stores text-to-speech (TTS) audio that has already been generated. Clearing the cache means any TTS audio will need to be regenerated by calling the API again the next time it is played. Your conversations and character data are not affected.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Logs">
    Contains context logs and load-balancing logs created by the app. These are diagnostic files and are safe to delete at any time without affecting app functionality or your data.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Character assets">
    Includes character card images and other visual assets downloaded for your characters. Clearing this category removes the locally cached copies; the assets will be re-downloaded the next time they are needed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Clearing cached data

Tap the category you want to clear, then confirm the action when prompted. You can clear one category at a time or clear all clearable categories together.

<Warning>
  The **Data** category — which contains your chat logs, character definitions, and world book entries — is intentionally excluded from the cleanup options. Tavo does not allow bulk deletion of this data from the storage screen to protect your conversations.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  If you use TTS frequently, the cache can grow quickly. Clearing it periodically is a good habit if you are running low on storage, since the audio will simply be regenerated on next playback.
</Tip>
