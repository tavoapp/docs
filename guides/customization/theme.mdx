Tavo's theme system gives you full control over the visual style of every chat session. You can apply one of the built-in official themes instantly, or create your own by copying an official theme and customizing it to match your preferences.

## Opening theme settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sidebar">
    In the main chat screen, swipe right or tap the menu icon to open the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap More">
    At the bottom of the sidebar, tap **More** to expand the options menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Theme">
    From the expanded menu, tap **Theme** to enter the theme management screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Applying a theme

On the theme management screen you will see two sections: **Official Themes** and **My Themes**. Tap any theme card to preview and apply it immediately. The change takes effect across all your chats.

<Tip>
  Official themes are a great way to explore what's possible. Apply a few to get a feel for the different styles before creating your own.
</Tip>

## Creating a custom theme

You cannot edit an official theme directly, but you can copy one as a starting point.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy an official theme">
    On any official theme card, tap the **Copy** or duplicate icon. Tavo creates a new editable theme in your **My Themes** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the theme editor">
    Tap the copied theme card to open the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust the visual elements">
    The editor exposes four categories of customizable elements:

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="Chat background and status bar">
        Set a solid color, gradient, or custom image for the chat background. You can also control the status bar color to match or contrast your background.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Message bubble style and font">
        Choose the shape, color, and opacity of message bubbles for both your messages and the character's replies. Adjust the font family and size used inside bubbles.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Character avatar display style">
        Control how the character's avatar appears alongside messages — for example, whether it shows as a circle or square, and how large it is relative to the bubble.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Inner monologue / thought bubble style">
        Customize the visual treatment of the character's inner monologue (思考过程 / thought bubbles), including bubble color, border, and font style.
      </Accordion>
    </AccordionGroup>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and apply">
    When you are satisfied with your changes, save the theme. It will appear in **My Themes** and be applied to your chats immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Custom themes are stored locally on your device. If you want to preserve them across devices or after reinstalling the app, make sure to include them in a [backup](/guides/customization/backup).
</Note>
