---

Custom shortcuts are pre-saved text snippets that appear as buttons above your keyboard during a chat. Tapping one instantly inserts the full text into the input field, saving you from typing the same phrases repeatedly. They are especially useful for common role-play actions, greetings, scene-setting prompts, or any text you find yourself entering often.

## Opening shortcut settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sidebar">
    Swipe right or tap the menu icon to open the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap More, then Settings">
    Tap **More** at the bottom of the sidebar, then select **Settings** from the expanded menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Chat Settings">
    Inside Settings, tap the **Chat Settings** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Custom Shortcuts">
    Tap **Custom Chat Shortcuts** to open the shortcut management screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Creating a shortcut

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Add">
    On the Custom Chat Shortcuts screen, tap the **Add** button (usually a `+` icon).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a label">
    Give the shortcut a short, memorable label — for example, `Greet` or `*hugs*`. This label appears on the button above the keyboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the content">
    Type the full text that should be inserted when the shortcut is tapped — for example, `Hello! How are you feeling today?` or `*wraps arms around you warmly*`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the shortcut">
    Tap **Save** or **Confirm**. The shortcut now appears in your list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Enabling and disabling shortcuts

Each shortcut in the list has a toggle switch on the right side. Only shortcuts with the toggle turned **on** will appear above the keyboard during a chat. Disable shortcuts you do not need right now without deleting them, so they are easy to re-enable later.

## Using shortcuts in a chat

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open any chat and tap the input field">
    When the keyboard appears, your enabled shortcuts will display as buttons in the bar just above the keyboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap a shortcut button">
    Tap the button with the label you want. The full text content of that shortcut is instantly inserted into the input field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit if needed, then send">
    You can edit the inserted text before sending, making it easy to use a shortcut as a starting template rather than a fixed phrase.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **Ideas for useful shortcuts:**
  - Common role-play action emotes: `*smiles softly*`, `*looks away*`
  - Frequent scene prompts: `Let's continue from where we left off.`
  - Macro combos that combine an action with dialogue: `*pauses* Wait — can you say that again?`
  - Language-specific phrases you use in multilingual chats
</Tip>
