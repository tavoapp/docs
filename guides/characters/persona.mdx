In Tavo, a **user persona** (also called a user identity) defines who *you* are in the conversation. While the character card controls the AI's behavior, your persona controls how the AI perceives and relates to you — your name, your role, and your relationship to the character. Setting a thoughtful persona is one of the most effective ways to shape the tone and direction of a chat.

The active persona name is what `{{user}}` resolves to anywhere it appears in character cards and prompts.

## What a persona does

When you start a chat, the AI doesn't know anything about you unless you tell it. Your persona fills in that gap. A well-written persona:

- Tells the AI what relationship you and the character have before the first message
- Anchors the AI's responses to a consistent dynamic (a friend, a rival, a stranger)
- Lets you maintain different identities for different characters or scenarios
- Prevents the AI from defaulting to a generic or inconsistent portrayal of "the user"

## Creating a persona

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    Tap **Menu** in the app's navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap More">
    In the menu, tap **More** to expand the additional settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select User Identity">
    Tap **User Identity** to open your list of personas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap New">
    Tap **New** to create a persona. A persona is your identity in a chat — who you are as the other person in the conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details and save">
    Tap the avatar area to set a profile photo, then fill in your **name** and a **persona description**. Tap **Save** when you are done. Your new persona is ready to use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Switching between personas

If you have multiple personas, you can switch the active one at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open User Identity">
    Go to **Menu → More → User Identity**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the options for a persona">
    Tap the three-dot menu (**...**) to the right of the persona you want to activate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set as default">
    Select **Set as default**. The chosen persona becomes your active identity for new chats. You can also edit or delete personas from this same menu.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Writing an effective persona description

Your persona description should answer two questions in one or two sentences:

1. **Who are you in relation to the character?** (stranger, childhood friend, sworn enemy, apprentice, colleague)
2. **What is your situation when the story begins?** (why you are here, what you know, what you want)

Optionally, add a sentence about the **emotional tone** of the relationship — tense, warm, cautious, playful.

<Tip>
Write the description in third person using `{{user}}` in place of your name. This matches how character cards reference you and ensures consistent handling by the AI.
</Tip>

## Persona examples by relationship type

The following examples show how different persona descriptions produce very different conversational dynamics.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Intimate relationship — emotional bond">
    **Persona description:**

    > {{user}} is {{char}}'s childhood friend and the one person she trusts completely. The two of you share an unspoken understanding; your interactions are natural, warm, and close.

    **Effect:** The character treats you with unconditional trust, shares personal thoughts and secrets, uses affectionate terms, and maintains a tone of quiet care throughout the conversation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Professional relationship — goal-oriented">
    **Persona description:**

    > {{user}} is the royal court's appointed apprentice and supervisor assigned to {{char}}, a legendary mage. Your role is to document her research and ensure she does not cross into forbidden magic. The relationship is careful and mutually watchful.

    **Effect:** The character addresses you in a teacher-student or overseen-overseer dynamic, mixing instruction with occasional posturing, and creates a clear sense of shared purpose alongside an underlying power tension.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Adversarial relationship — conflict and rivalry">
    **Persona description:**

    > {{user}} is the detective who has foiled {{char}}'s plans more than once and has finally cornered her. You are evenly matched opponents who respect each other's skill even as you stand on opposite sides.

    **Effect:** The character's dialogue carries provocation, sharp wit, and flashes of reluctant admiration. Exchanges feel like a contest of intellect with real stakes, and the tension stays high.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Unknown relationship — mystery and discovery">
    **Persona description:**

    > {{user}} woke up in a forest with no memory beyond their own name. You've just encountered {{char}}, who is curious about this unexpected stranger but wary of your presence.

    **Effect:** The character actively tries to read you — asking questions, watching your responses closely, and withholding trust. The dynamic is suited to mystery-driven stories where the relationship evolves through discovery.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Situational — specific scenario entry">
    **Persona description:**

    > {{user}} is a soldier who has just returned from the front, tired and carrying wounds. You've stopped at the remote inn run by {{char}}, looking for a quiet place to rest and a drink in peace.

    **Effect:** The character (the innkeeper) responds to your visible state — showing concern, restraint, or quiet curiosity — and the conversation opens inside a fully realized scene rather than a blank context.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The `{{user}}` macro

Anywhere `{{user}}` appears in a character card — in the description, scenario, dialogue examples, or prompts — Tavo replaces it with the name from your active persona. This means a character card written with `{{user}}` automatically adapts to whoever is chatting with it, making the same card feel personal for every user.

<Info>
If you have no persona set, `{{user}}` typically resolves to a default placeholder. For the most immersive experience, always set an active persona before starting a chat.
</Info>
